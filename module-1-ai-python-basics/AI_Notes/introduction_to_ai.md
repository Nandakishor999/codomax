# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI)

## What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating machines and software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI systems can learn from information, recognize patterns, understand language, solve problems, and make decisions.

### Simple Example

A chatbot can understand a user's question and provide an appropriate answer. This is an example of Artificial Intelligence.

## What Can AI Do?

AI can perform many intelligent tasks, such as:

- Understanding human language
- Recognizing images and faces
- Recognizing speech
- Making predictions
- Solving problems
- Recommending products or content
- Generating text, images, audio, and code
- Detecting unusual or suspicious activities

## How Does AI Work?

A simple AI system generally follows these steps:

1. **Collect Data** – Gather information required for the task.
2. **Process Data** – Clean and prepare the data.
3. **Learn Patterns** – Use algorithms or models to identify patterns.
4. **Make Decisions** – Use the learned patterns to produce an output.
5. **Improve** – Evaluate the results and improve the system when possible.

## Types of AI

AI can be broadly discussed in terms of its capabilities:

### 1. Narrow AI

Narrow AI is designed to perform a specific task.

Examples:

- Voice assistants
- Recommendation systems
- Spam filters
- Face recognition systems

Most AI applications available today are examples of Narrow AI.

### 2. General AI

Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) refers to a hypothetical AI system that could perform a wide range of intellectual tasks at a human-like level.

AGI has not been achieved.

### 3. Super AI

Super AI refers to a hypothetical system that would surpass human intelligence across many areas.

It is currently a theoretical concept.

## AI in Everyday Life

We interact with AI in many common applications:

- Google Search
- YouTube recommendations
- Online shopping recommendations
- Voice assistants
- Maps and route suggestions
- Email spam filtering
- Face unlock on smartphones
- Chatbots
- Generative AI tools

## Why is AI Important?

AI can help organizations automate repetitive tasks, analyze large amounts of data, identify patterns, support decision-making, and create new products and services.

## Key Takeaway

Artificial Intelligence is the broader field of creating systems that can perform tasks associated with human intelligence. Machine Learning and Deep Learning are important technologies used to build many modern AI systems.
